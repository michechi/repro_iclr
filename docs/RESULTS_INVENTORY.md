# Results inventory and interpretation

This inventory describes the aggregate evidence packaged with the anonymous artifact. Paths below are relative to the artifact root unless linked. The package is a **sanitized derivative of retained experiment outputs**, not a new run or an independent re-evaluation of every prediction. Numerical results retain their recorded precision. Machine paths and operational metadata have been removed or made relative. A source path in a table is a provenance reference; it does not imply that its checkpoint, original log, or predictions are distributed.

The manuscript snapshot mirrors the current working draft and still contains unresolved discrepancies. Use the measured files described here for quantitative claims, and read [MANUSCRIPT_GAPS.md](MANUSCRIPT_GAPS.md) before reusing a manuscript table. A missing, cancelled, dry-run, smoke, or skipped experiment supplies no production estimate. Repeated evaluations of one fitted model are not independent training seeds.

## Large-model KIP-4 data efficiency

[summary.csv](../results/kip_m4_data_efficiency_20260920_seed9950/summary.csv) contains 24 planned rows: **16 completed fits** and **eight `skipped_saturated` rows**. The four arms are Llama-3.1-8B LoRA, Llama-3.1-8B full fine-tuning, Qwen2.5-14B QLoRA, and Qwen3.8-27B QLoRA. Each completed fraction uses one seed, 9950, and 4K, 40K, 120K, or 200K training examples. All retain the full fixed 50K validation and 50K test sets. Training subsets are stratified and shared across arms at a given fraction; they are not guaranteed to be nested across fractions.

Each arm reaches recorded test AUC 1.0 at 200K, and the 300K/400K fractions were **not run**. The stopping amendment used perfect test AUC to stop training and skip larger fractions. These results therefore have **test-informed stopping and budget selection**; they do not establish an independent held-out estimate or an unbiased 200K sample-complexity threshold. Validation still selected checkpoints and F1 thresholds. The original Qwen27 4K/40K/120K fits precede the amendment; their original metric records are retained. Qwen27's 200K endpoint is the separately finalized, validation-selected epoch-9 checkpoint in `fraction_0.50/final_evaluation_20260921/`.

Do not extend these curves to 300K/400K, add seed uncertainty bands, call the endpoints three-seed means, or relabel the 200K scores as measured 400K results. The bundled KIP fraction table alone cannot regenerate an all-task, all-model OC/GPC/KIP learning-curve figure.

## Frozen few-shot evaluation

## OC fits and attention

Five completed OC audits are included: full-fine-tuned BERT-base and RoBERTa-base, LoRA Llama-3.1-8B, QLoRA Qwen3.8-27B on deterministic OC, and a separate QLoRA Qwen3.8-27B noisy-OC fit. Encoder/Llama fits use 200K training examples; Qwen fits use 400K. These are distinct task-specific initializations from the pretrained bases, not continuations of KIP training. Their per-fit/per-audit metric JSONs are under the corresponding encoder, Llama, Qwen OC, and Qwen noisy-OC result roots.

Both Qwen fits completed six epochs under an explicit epoch cap, while retaining the original **20-epoch learning-rate schedule**. They are not equivalent to fresh runs whose scheduler was configured for six epochs. Validation selected epoch 5 for deterministic OC and epoch 6 for noisy OC. Both are **single-seed** results and record `test_informed_stopping: false`.

| Qwen OC fit | Full-test AUC | F1 with validation-selected threshold | Test threshold |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Deterministic, selected epoch 5 | 0.9999999980639958 | 0.9999657193788352 | 0.10 |
| Noisy, selected epoch 6 | 0.6653328762089209 | 0.586908597591996 | approximately 0.24 |

The noisy fit's F1 at a fixed threshold of 0.5 is 0.5709767599. Its full-test AUC uses observed noisy labels; attention input perturbations instead assess the latent clean rule. These targets and thresholds must remain explicit.

The publication [OC profile CSV](../paper/figures/attention_oc_profiles_20260925.csv) contains 264 model/layer/metric aggregate rows. Its companion JSON records aggregation. All five audits use the same 1,000 held-out synthetic examples, including 292 compliant examples; lag-category enrichment has only 465 eligible examples. Enrichment is averaged over eligible examples and heads, and its categories do not form an additive partition. Perturbation results use a fixed 200-example diagnostic set: label-flipping permutations were found for 192 examples; the noisy fit followed 187 of those flips. This conditional subset is not an unbiased performance estimate.

## Unavailable, incomplete, and excluded evidence

- **Llama-70B KIP:** the planned run was cancelled before model preparation/training; there is no completed production endpoint. Its pending summary or preflight cannot support a KIP success or failure claim.
- **Llama-8B full KIP-6:** the earlier run produced only partial validation history and smoke evidence, and those files are not distributed; no completed production test metric is supplied.
- **40-shot Qwen thinking:** the packaged task summary remains incomplete; completed prior nonthinking rows are comparisons, not thinking results.
- **Mechanism-identification per-run outputs:** `parity_decomp_dryrun/summary_table.tex` is a dry-run marker, and the ladder, held-out-rule and parity-decomposition CSVs are not archived; the included `mechanism_id_reference/report.md` and plots transcribe those results but are not run outputs. The curriculum per-seed outputs and the OC-Noisy and L1 OC-Deterministic cells of the all-lag stacked baseline have no archived source at all; only the three all-lag cells in `feature_table_completed_20260918/new_metrics.csv` are measured.
- **Stochastic sensitivity sweep fits:** no per-configuration, per-seed fits with label noise are archived; the deterministic single-seed summaries in `results/sensitivity/` are a different protocol and supply no AUC/AUC* ratio.
- **OC hole main neural fits:** the aggregate model summary is empty and required configurations remain missing; completed control results do not fill those gaps.
- **Full historical learning-curve grid:** complete source measurements for all manuscript OC/GPC/model/fraction combinations are not available in this package. The missing main/all-model curve images must not be reconstructed by interpolating or simulating points.
- **GPC simulated table cells and unsupported manuscript SDs:** text in the manuscript is not promoted to a measured result. See [MANUSCRIPT_GAPS.md](MANUSCRIPT_GAPS.md).
- **Smoke tests, pilots, failed/unfinished attempts, operational logs, generated reasoning transcripts, checkpoint tensors and per-example predictions:** these are not released as production scientific results. Where a mixed historical summary retains a status field, only explicitly completed production rows are valid evidence.

The three publication attention CSVs are sufficient to replot their aggregate figures without performing model inference or reading clinical patient records. Recomputing model metrics from individual predictions is intentionally outside the aggregate release; retraining and original-data access are separate reproduction steps.
